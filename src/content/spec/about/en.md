
About Me...

I first thought about starting a blog a year ago, when I was still a freshman. However, I put the idea aside and eventually forgot about it. Now, it has suddenly crossed my mind again, so here I am.

I do some software development with my genius programmer, Codex, and occasionally tinker with microcontrollers—mostly just getting LEDs to light up. I don’t really have any special hobbies. I usually read web novels, especially satisfying power-fantasy stories, and listen to music—mostly Khalil Fong.

In the future, I hope to learn more skills, improve my technical abilities, and document some of the things I learn and create on this blog.
